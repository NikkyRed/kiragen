ABOUT ME

Based in Belgrade, Serbia. Following crypto since 2017, working in Web3 professionally since 2022, 6 years in digital marketing overall. I build and lead social media functions for crypto and gamedev products at scale – from strategy architecture to team management and measurable business results.

My approach is analytics-first: I work with hypotheses, test against data, and adjust based on what actually performs. At Blum, one content post drove 300K+ new wallet connections directly attributed to the campaign. I've led teams of 5–10 people, run cross-regional operations across Global and CIS markets – Turkey, Brazil, Africa, Asia, and beyond, and operated across a combined social footprint of 30M+ followers on a single project.

What I'm looking for: a product I believe in and a role where scope, responsibility and pay line up. I'm equally comfortable leading a team or owning channels hands-on – the product matters more to me than the title. I measure my work by one thing: whether the business grows, because when the business does well, so do I.
