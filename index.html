ABOUT ME

Based in Belgrade, Serbia. Following crypto since 2017, working in Web3 professionally since 2022, 6 years in digital marketing overall. I build social functions as systems, not just feeds: I take an unstructured communication setup, understand the product and the market, shape the narrative, and turn it into a multi-platform operation with clear processes, a team and measurable impact.

I connect product, audience, content, distribution and analytics. At Blum, one educational post drove 300K+ new wallet connections; at Dlicom, I built the whole social function from zero across 10 platforms and the founder's personal brand. I've led teams of 5–10 people, run regional operations across Turkey, Brazil, Africa, Asia and CIS, and worked on a footprint of 30M+ followers on a single project. I stay hands-on: I can write the thread, brief the visual and edit the video myself.

What I'm looking for: a Head / Lead / Senior role in social, content or Web3 marketing – open to product-marketing-adjacent roles too – with a product I believe in, where scope, responsibility and pay line up. I'm equally comfortable leading a team or owning channels hands-on – the product matters more to me than the title. I measure my work by one thing: whether the business grows, because when the business does well, so do I.
